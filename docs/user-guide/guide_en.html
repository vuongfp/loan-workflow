Introduction and workflow overview

Loan Workflow manages personal loan applications (home purchase, purchase in a developer project, secured personal loans, car loans) through these steps: Sale enters the application, the Sale levels check it, CED reviews and scores it, two approvers approve it, then CRA prepares the contracts and disburses.

At any moment an application has one status and one handler. Only the handler can move it to the next step. Every step is recorded: who moved it, when, and with what comment.

Signing in and the home page

Signing in

Open the system's address in your browser (the demo runs at http://localhost:8000), enter your username and password, and click Log in.

Demo accounts The demo has an account for each role: sale1, sale2, salelead, salesupport, saledirector, headpfs, ced1, ced2, cedadmin, approver1, approver2, cra1, craadmin. They all share the password set in DEMO_USER_PASSWORD in the .env file.

Home page (Dashboard)

After signing in you see your own home page:

  • Four figures at the top: applications waiting for you, your overdue applications, open applications you can see, and applications completed in the last 30 days.
  • My work queue: the applications waiting for you, earliest due date first. Past due dates are red. Click an application number to open it.
  • Open applications by status: how many open applications are at each step.

The left sidebar has Dashboard, Loan applications and Applicants. The search box at the top (shortcut Ctrl K) jumps to a page. Click your name at the bottom to change your password, switch between light and dark mode, or sign out.

Due dates Each time an application moves to a new step, the new handler has 3 days for it (a demo setting). Overdue applications are red on the home page and in the list.

Finding and opening applications

Click Loan applications in the sidebar. You only see the applications you may see: those you created, are handling or have handled, and your team's applications if you are a team admin.

  • Search: type an application number, an applicant's name or ID number, then press Enter.
  • Columns: number, primary applicant, product, loan amount, status (coloured by step group), current handler (With), due date (Due) and last change.
  • Sorting: click a column heading.
  • Filters: click Filters to filter by status, approval type, handler or creation date range, then click Apply Filters.

Application numbers come in two forms: APmmyy… while the application is a Sale draft, and DS.<product>.<year>.<sequence> (for example DS.SS.26.0004), given automatically when it reaches CED.

Sale: creating and completing an application

Creating an application

  1. In Loan applications, click the + button at the top right.
  2. Click Create application to confirm. The system creates a draft with you as submitter and handler, with an empty loan request and an empty collateral.

The application page

The application page has a tab for each part: General, Loan request, Applicants, Collaterals, Credit decision (CED), Conditions, Documents, Loan contract (CRA), Contract documents, and under the ··· menu Sale parties and Workflow history. The buttons at the top: Change status (workflow), CRR scoring, Credit memo (PDF).

The General › Overview tab shows the status, handler, due date, primary applicant and loan at a glance.

Who may edit which part? Each part can only be edited by its role, at the right step. Sale edits the Sale part while the application is at a Sale step (Open, or when sent back). At other steps the fields are read-only. See the table in the Appendix.

Loan request

Choose the product and sub-product, enter the amount, tenor (months), loan program, pricing scheme, purpose, loan-to-value (MOA %) and any deviations. Click Save and continue editing. The monthly installments (highest, lowest, average) are calculated when you save.

Tip Changing the product updates the collateral type to match (car, property with title deed, property in a project).

Applicants

The Applicants tab lists the primary applicant and co-applicants with each one's monthly income. Add a row to enter a co-applicant. Click a name to open the applicant's page.

The applicant page holds personal details, addresses, residence and relationship with the bank. The unaccented name and addresses used on documents are filled in automatically from the Vietnamese ones.

Income and credit bureau (CIC)

The tabs on the applicant page hold each income source and each existing debt reported by the credit bureau:

After each save, the Income summary (calculated) tab shows the applicant's total income and existing debt payments.

Collaterals

Enter the collateral type and the purchase-contract or estimated value, then open Property (house, land, project apartment) or Car for details. An application can have several collaterals. The legal data for the contracts is entered later by CRA, on each collateral's own page (section 9.2).

Documents

Add a row, choose the document type, choose the file, then save. Files can only be opened by people who may see the application.

Repayment capacity (Credit assessment)

The General › Credit assessment tab shows total income, existing debt payments, living cost, DSR, net surplus (NST), customer segment and the CRR score. All of it is recalculated every time the application is saved.

Main formulas DSR = (this loan's payment + existing debt payments) / total income. NST = income − debt payments − living cost. The default living cost depends on the number of applicants; administrators set it in Credit settings.

Moving an application to the next step

When your part is done, click Change status on the application page. The page shows:

  • A summary of the application at the top: applicant, loan, income, DSR, CRR score and due date, so you can decide without going back.
  • The steps you can take at the current status, one card each, with a coloured left edge: Move on moves the application forward (open by default), Send back returns it for changes, End rejects or cancels it. The last two are folded to avoid mistakes; click the title to open one.
  1. Open the card of the step you want.
  2. Choose the receiver in Hand over to. The system suggests a person; for CED and CRA it suggests the team member with the fewest open applications (workload balancing).
  3. Write a comment. For sending back or rejecting, a reason is required and the receiver sees it in the history.
  4. When CED or an approver rejects, choose a rejection reason. When CED sends back to Sale, a QC item can be chosen.
  5. Click the step's button. The status changes, the receiver gets the application and an email (if they have an email address).
No steps shown? You only see steps while you are the handler and your group may take a step at the current status. If someone else is handling the application, the page says there is no step for you.

Sale Leader, Sale Support and exception approval

Applications handed to you appear in My work queue on the home page.

  • Sale Leader: open and check the application, then Endorse to Sale Support; or Request Update to send it back to Sale; or Reject.
  • Sale Support: check completeness, then Submit to CED. For an exception, Submit to Head of Sale. Request Sale Update asks Sale for more; the application comes back to Sale Support after Sale updates it.
  • Head of Sale → Head of PFS: approve the exception; Head of PFS clicks Approve to send the application to CED.

CED: credit review and CRR scoring

When an application reaches CED, the system gives it a DS.… number and hands it to a CED officer (suggested by workload).

Credit decision

In the Credit decision (CED) tab, enter the approved amount, valuations, error codes, the CED recommendation and the valuation report file. The comments of Approver 1 and 2 are kept here too.

CRR scoring

  1. Click CRR scoring on the application page.
  2. Enter the bureau and account inputs: age of account, conduct of account, whether a bureau report exists, number of credit institutions, defaults in the last 12 and 60 months.
  3. Click Calculate score. The other inputs (age, marital status, education, income, DSR, loan, LTV…) come from the application.

The result shows the total score, the band and the points of each criterion. Others can see the result but not change it.

Approval conditions

In the Conditions tab, add the conditions before or after disbursement. Two columns record that CED and CRA have followed each condition.

Moving on

Click Change status: Recommend to Approver 1 to submit for approval, Request Sale Update to ask Sale for more, or Reject with a reason.

Two-level approval

Approvers open the application from the home page, look at General › Credit assessment, CRR scoring and the Credit memo (PDF), write their comment in the Credit decision (CED) tab, then click Change status.

Four-eyes rule The second approver must be a different person from the first. The system does not let the first approver also give the second approval, and leaves them out of the list of receivers.

After the second approval the application returns to Sale in Post Approval. Sale clicks Request Docs Preparation to send it to CRA, or Valuation Review Request if CED should look at the valuation again.

CRA: contracts and disbursement

Loan contract terms

The Loan contract (CRA) tab: amount, credit limit (in figures and words), disbursement amount, interest rate, margin, early repayment fee, tenor, disbursement method, purpose and other conditions.

Collateral legal data

In the Collaterals tab, click a collateral's open icon to go to its own page. The top part is what Sale entered (read-only); the Contract data (CRA) part below is for CRA: land plot, map sheet, certificate, valuation, purchase contract, notarised transfer; for a car, the chassis and engine numbers and the dealer.

Contract documents

The Contract documents tab: add each document (credit agreement FA, mortgage agreement MA, tripartite agreement TA, valuation minutes), its number, signing date and signer. After saving, the Print column opens a PDF of the contract's parties section, filled in with the borrower's and the bank's details.

The next steps: CRA Preparation Complete → Sale Confirm Disbursement (or asks CED to check the conditions before disbursement) → CRA Confirm Disbursement Complete. The application becomes Complete.

Workflow history and the credit memo

Workflow history

The ··· › Workflow history tab lists every step, newest first: time, person, step, new status and comment.

Two kinds of history The History button in the top bar is the admin's log of data changes, not the application's workflow history.

Credit memo (PDF)

Click Credit memo (PDF) to open the credit memo: loan request, applicants, collateral, repayment capacity, CRR score, credit decision, approvers' comments, conditions and workflow history. Print or save it from the browser.

Team admins: workload and re-assignment

CED and CRA team admins see all their team's applications. Their home page adds a Team workload table: each officer's open applications and whether they receive new work.

  • Pausing new work: each officer has a Pause / Resume button on the home page (for example during leave). Paused officers are not suggested for new applications.
  • Re-assigning: on the Change status page, open Re-assign (team admin), choose the new handler, add a note and click Re-assign. The status does not change.

Adding a user

  1. Open Users, click +, enter a username and a first password.
  2. Enter the full name, tick Staff status (may use the system) and choose the group for the role (for example SaleTeam, CEDOfficer).
  3. For a CED or CRA officer, add a row in Officers so that they receive work automatically.
Changing the workflow Editing Workflow actions changes the steps users see immediately. Try changes in a test environment first.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I edit the application? It is not at your step, or the part belongs to another role. Check the status and handler in the General tab.

The step I need is not shown? Steps are only shown to the current handler in the right group. If the handler is wrong, ask the team admin to re-assign.

DSR, NST or installments did not change after editing? They are recalculated when you click Save. Save the page and open the tab again.

The CRR score says "Not scored yet"? CED has not scored yet. The score is calculated when CED clicks Calculate score.

Nobody to choose as receiver? No one in the receiving group is active or taking new work. Contact the team admin or an administrator.

An attachment says I have no permission? Only people who may see the application can open its documents.
